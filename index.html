How gameweek statistics work

Gameweek membership follows the official fixture assignment. A postponed fixture without an event keeps its last known gameweek until the official feed assigns it again. A later assignment moves it to that gameweek. Cancelled fixtures do not count as remaining or receive scores. A round stays provisional while any other fixture is unresolved.

Only confirmed completed results contribute scores. Missing forecasts receive the match mean for O Score only; accuracy and Brier use eligible submitted forecasts and valid probability samples. Competition standings and winners remain disabled until activation.

How these numbers work

O Score adds home-goal error, away-goal error, and one point when the predicted result is wrong. Lower is better. In full history, each missing forecast receives that match's mean earned O Score. The table separates submitted forecasts, missing forecasts, earned scores, mean-assigned scores and scored matches. A match with no earned scores has no O Score.

Correct-result and exact-score rates use only eligible forecasts. Mean-assigned O Scores never count as forecasts. Brier uses the existing three-outcome formula; its sample count includes only valid probability distributions. Full-history statistics include all retained completed results. The shared view includes results with eligible forecasts from all five agents, and Brier scores share a valid-probability denominator.

Competition standings use only matches kicking off on or after the recorded activation time. Earlier historical statistics do not determine competition standings.

A LITTLE CONTEXT

Football is unpredictable.
That’s the point.

Five forecasting agents evaluate the same recent league form, goals scored and conceded, and home advantage to offer their opinions on each result. Fixture data comes directly from Fantasy Premier League. Fixtures refresh daily. Forecasts update on Wednesdays for the next ten days, with failed requests eligible for scheduled retries before Thursday 09:00 London. Results and accuracy update as matches are played.

These are experimental estimates, not validated odds or guarantees. New forecasts prioritize the predicted result. Each provider estimates score probabilities; we add them up to choose the most likely result, then select the most likely score within that result. That score may differ from the most likely score overall. Earlier forecasts retain their original predictions. Result accuracy and exact score accuracy are measured separately. Injuries and confirmed lineups are not included. Fixtures can change.
