A LITTLE CONTEXT

Football is unpredictable.
That’s the point.

Jev evaluates recent league form, goals scored and conceded, and home advantage to estimate each result. Fixture data comes directly from Fantasy Premier League. Fixtures and predictions refresh once a day; results and accuracy update as matches are played.

These are experimental model estimates, not validated odds or guarantees. Exact scores and match outcomes are evaluated separately, so they may differ. Injuries and confirmed lineups are not included. Fixtures can change.
