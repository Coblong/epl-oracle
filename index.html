A LITTLE CONTEXT

Football is unpredictable.
That’s the point.

Jev and OpenAI Decisions evaluate the same recent league form, goals scored and conceded, and home advantage to offer two opinions on each result. Fixture data comes directly from Fantasy Premier League. Fixtures refresh daily. Forecasts update on Wednesdays for the next ten days, with failed requests eligible for scheduled retries before Thursday 09:00 London. Results and accuracy update as matches are played.

These are experimental model estimates, not validated odds or guarantees. Exact scores and match outcomes are evaluated separately, so they may differ. Injuries and confirmed lineups are not included. Fixtures can change.
