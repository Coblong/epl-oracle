How these numbers work

Each completed match counts once per provider, using its latest eligible prediction made before kickoff. Cancelled matches are excluded. All matches includes all retained Neon results, regardless of the Results page or season. Same matches includes only matches predicted by all five agents.

Correct result measures home wins, draws and away wins. Correct score measures the exact score. Brier score measures the accuracy of the three result probabilities: 0 is best and 2 is worst. Probabilities are normalized to remove small rounding differences. Missing or invalid probabilities are excluded from Brier scoring only. In Same matches, all Brier scores use the same matches with valid probabilities from every agent; their sample counts are shown.

A LITTLE CONTEXT

Football is unpredictable.
That’s the point.

Jev and OpenAI evaluate the same recent league form, goals scored and conceded, and home advantage to offer two opinions on each result. Fixture data comes directly from Fantasy Premier League. Fixtures refresh daily. Forecasts update on Wednesdays for the next ten days, with failed requests eligible for scheduled retries before Thursday 09:00 London. Results and accuracy update as matches are played.

These are experimental estimates, not validated odds or guarantees. New forecasts prioritize the predicted result. Each provider estimates score probabilities; we add them up to choose the most likely result, then select the most likely score within that result. That score may differ from the most likely score overall. Earlier forecasts retain their original predictions. Result accuracy and exact score accuracy are measured separately. Injuries and confirmed lineups are not included. Fixtures can change.
